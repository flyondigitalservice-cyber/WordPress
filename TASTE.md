# Hadhrami — Taste

The rules this site is designed by. Read before changing anything visual.

## Who it is for

Men in Indian cities buying a suit, a shirt or a thobe for a date that matters:
a wedding, an interview, a festival. They want to see the garment, trust the
maker, and know when it arrives. Most are on a phone.

## The one promise

**Made to measure. Delivered in 2 days.**
Every page should make that promise findable within one screen. Never promise
a time the workshop cannot keep.

## Character

A confident family tailoring house, not a tech start-up. Warm, direct, a
little proud. Colour comes from our own logo: the sunset, the sea, the ink.

## Colour

| Name    | Hex       | Used for                                         |
|---------|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Ink     | `#14110F` | Text, primary buttons, footer                    |
| Paper   | `#FFFDF8` | Page background                                  |
| Cream   | `#F7EFE0` | Quiet sections, cards                            |
| Saffron | `#F4A300` | The promise, highlights, one block per screen    |
| Sea     | `#0C4A6E` | Deep sections, trust, process                    |
| Sky     | `#2E90D1` | Small accents on Sea                             |
| Clay    | `#C2410C` | Links on light backgrounds, alerts, sale         |

- Colour is used in **solid blocks**, never gradients, glows or blurred blobs.
- At most two strong colours in one screen. Saffron is loud: one block at a time.
- Text on Saffron is always Ink. Text on Sea is always Paper.

## Type

- **Bodoni Moda** for headlines. Large, tight, sentence case. Italic for one
  emphasised phrase per headline, never more.
- **Jost** for everything else: navigation, buttons, product names, body.
- Small labels are uppercase with wide tracking; nothing else is uppercase.
- Body text is never lighter than regular weight and never below 15px.

## Layout

- Left-aligned by default. Centre only short, single-line section titles.
- Sections are numbered (01, 02, 03) so the page reads like a printed lookbook.
- Asymmetry over symmetry: a big column beside a small one, not three equal cards.
- Square corners on photos and blocks. Fully round only on buttons and chips.
- Generous space between sections, tight space inside them.

## Photography

- The garment leads. On a card: the suit first, the cloth second.
- Product photos sit on colour blocks, not on white boxes.
- No stock lifestyle photos unless they show our own work.

## Words

- Plain English. Say "delivered in 2 days", not "expedited fulfilment".
- Product names describe the product: "Navy Chalk Stripe Suit".
- No invented reviews, awards or numbers. If we cannot prove it, we do not say it.
- Buttons say what happens next: "Add to bag", "Get a quote on WhatsApp".

## Interaction

- Every tap target is at least 44px.
- Motion is short and has a reason: a photo eases on hover, a drawer slides in.
  Nothing bounces, spins or fades in on scroll for decoration.
- The bag, search and saved items are always one tap away.
- Honour "reduce motion".

## Never

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, glowing shadows, emoji as icons.
- Three identical cards with an icon, a title and two lines of grey text.
- Everything centred. Everything uppercase. Everything animated.
- A second accent colour invented for one page.
