# Hadhrami — Taste

The rules this site is designed by. Read before changing anything visual.
References: Suitsupply and Hockerty listing pages.

## Who it is for

Men in Indian cities buying a suit, a shirt or a thobe for a date that matters:
a wedding, an interview, a festival. They want to see the garment clearly,
trust the maker, and know when it arrives. Most are on a phone.

## The one promise

**Made to measure. Delivered in 2 days.**
It lives in the top bar on every page and in the first hero slide. Never
promise a time the workshop cannot keep.

## Character

A classic tailoring house with a young, clear way of selling. Quiet
confidence: the photographs and the cloth do the talking, the interface
stays out of the way.

## Colour

| Name   | Hex       | Used for                                        |
|--------|-----------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Ink    | `#141414` | Text, buttons, top bar, footer                  |
| Paper  | `#FFFFFF` | Page background                                 |
| Stone  | `#F6F4F0` | Quiet sections, contact card, delivery note     |
| Photo  | `#EFEEEB` | Behind every product photograph                 |
| Line   | `#E4E1DC` | Hairlines between sections and items            |
| Muted  | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text                                  |

- No brand colour in the interface. The logo carries the saffron and sea.
- Dark only where a campaign photograph sets the mood (hero, editorial split).

## Type

- **GFS Didot** for headlines, brand name and large numbers. One weight only
  (regular): never bold it, never italicise it.
- **Jost** for everything else: navigation, buttons, product names, body.
- Small uppercase labels with wide tracking for section kickers only.
- Body text 15px, regular weight.

## Layout

- Product grids run edge to edge with 4px gaps. Four across on desktop,
  three on tablet, two on phones.
- Listings open with a row of small photo sub-categories, then the title on
  the left and one "Filter & Sort" button on the right.
- Cards: photograph, then name and price on one line, then the cloth.
- Square corners. Buttons have a 2px radius; only the filter pill is round.
- Generous space between sections, hairlines rather than boxes.

## Photography

- The garment leads. Suit first, cloth second; the cloth appears on hover.
- Product photos sit on light grey. Campaign photos are warm and dark.
- No stock lifestyle photos unless they show our own work.

## Words

- Plain English. "Delivered in 2 days", not "expedited fulfilment".
- Product names describe the product: "Navy Chalk Stripe Suit".
- No invented reviews, awards or numbers.
- Buttons say what happens next: "Add to bag", "Show 38 products".

## Interaction

- Every tap target is at least 44px.
- Filters live in one drawer; the button follows you down long listings.
- Motion is short and has a reason. Nothing bounces or autoplays sound.
- Honour "reduce motion".

## Never

- Colourful blocks, gradients, glows, emoji as icons.
- Badges repeated on every card.
- Bold or italic GFS Didot.
- Everything centred. Everything uppercase.
