# Pricing page: bombaymarketing.in/pricing/

## Slug recommendation: `/pricing/`

**Use `/pricing/`, not `/ecommerce-marketing-pricing-mumbai/`.**

1. **The page serves every service.** It covers store builds, marketplace and quick-commerce onboarding, SEO, ads, social and packaging. A long slug about "ecommerce marketing" covers only part of that.
2. **The slug is a weak ranking signal; the title and content do the work.** "Mumbai", "ecommerce" and "agency pricing" belong in the title tag, H1 and body copy, where they carry far more weight than in a URL.
3. **It is what people and AI assistants guess.** Visitors type `/pricing`, and assistants checking an agency's costs look for it first. A predictable URL gets more direct and referred visits.
4. **It is stable.** If you add services or cities, `/pricing/` stays correct. A keyword slug would need a redirect.
5. **Cleaner sitelinks and anchor text.** "Pricing" is the label Google uses for sitelinks, and you can link to it from navigation without the anchor text looking stuffed.

The one case for the long slug is if you later create separate city pricing pages. If that happens, keep `/pricing/` as the hub and add children such as `/pricing/quick-commerce/`.

## What drives the price

**1. Catalogue size**
Twenty SKUs and two thousand SKUs are different projects. Every product needs a title, description, images, attributes, barcode, tax code and, on marketplaces, category mapping. Up to about 50 SKUs we can work largely by hand. Beyond that we build feeds and templates, which costs more to set up and less per SKU afterwards.

**2. Number of marketplaces and platforms**
Each platform has its own onboarding, catalogue format, fee structure, ad console and reporting. Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart are five separate workloads, not one. We price onboarding per platform and add a management fee for each platform we run monthly. Starting with one or two is usually cheaper and smarter.

**3. Ad budget**
Managing ₹30,000 a month of ad spend is different from managing ₹10 lakh. Bigger budgets need more campaigns, more creative, more testing and more reporting. Our management fee is a flat retainer up to a spend threshold, then moves to a percentage of spend. The ad budget itself goes straight to the platform; it is never part of our fee.

**4. Content volume**
Content is the hardest cost to see in advance. A monthly plan with eight social posts and two blog articles is a different job from daily reels, product shoots and twelve SEO articles. We price content by volume and format. Video and photo shoots are quoted separately because location, props and models vary so much.

**5. Integration complexity**
A Shopify store with standard apps is quick. A store that must sync stock with a warehouse system, push orders to Tally or an ERP, connect to several marketplaces and run custom checkout logic needs development and testing. Integration work is where projects overrun, so we scope it in writing before quoting.

**6. Starting point and compliance**
Brands with GST, FSSAI, trademark, GS1 barcodes and compliant packaging ready can be onboarded quickly. Brands that need these sorted first take longer, and some work, such as packaging redesign, becomes part of the project. We tell you on the first call which items are missing.

## How a project runs

**Step 1: Discovery and quote (week 1)**
A 45-minute call, then we review your store, listings, documents and numbers. You get a written scope with a fixed quote within the published range, or a reason it falls outside it.

**Step 2: Setup (weeks 2–5)**
Store build, platform onboarding, tracking and catalogue work. A standard Shopify store usually takes three to four weeks; a store with integrations, five to eight. Marketplace and quick-commerce timelines depend on platform approvals, which we cannot control.

**Step 3: Launch (weeks 5–8)**
Go-live, first stock shipments, first campaigns. We watch daily for the first two weeks: availability, listing errors, ad spend and conversion.

**Step 4: Optimise and report (ongoing, monthly)**
Monthly report and call. We adjust spend, content and catalogue based on contribution, not vanity metrics. Every quarter we review whether the retainer is still the right size, up or down.

## Cheaper alternatives, and when they're the right call

**Do it yourself on Shopify.** If you have under 50 products, time to learn, and no complex integrations, Shopify's own themes and guides will get you a working store for the cost of the subscription. That is the right call for many first-time founders.

**Hire a freelancer.** For a single defined job, such as a store build, a pack design or a month of listings, a good freelancer is often cheaper than an agency. You manage them, and you carry the risk if they disappear.

**Use the platform's own onboarding.** Blinkit's Seller Hub is self-serve and Amazon has its own seller support and service provider network. If your documents and packaging are already in order, you may not need us for onboarding at all.

**Hire us when** you need several channels coordinated, you do not have time to manage freelancers, or a mistake would cost more than our fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is there no fixed price?

Because two brands asking for "a Shopify store" or "Blinkit onboarding" rarely need the same work. Catalogue size, integrations, platforms, compliance and content volume change the effort by several times. A fixed price would either overcharge small projects or force us to cut corners on large ones. We give you a range here and a fixed written quote after discovery.

### Do you take a revenue share?

Not as standard. [CONFIRM WITH OWNER] Revenue share sounds aligned, but it makes our fee depend on things neither of us fully controls, such as stock availability and platform fees, and it complicates your accounts. For performance marketing above a spend threshold, we charge a percentage of ad spend, which is a different thing: it is tied to the work we do, not to your sales.

### What's the minimum ad budget you'd recommend?

For Meta and Google, we suggest at least ₹30,000–₹50,000 a month of ad spend so campaigns get enough data to learn within a few weeks. [CONFIRM WITH OWNER] Below that, our management fee becomes a large share of the total and you may be better off running simple campaigns yourself. For quick-commerce in-app ads, start smaller and scale once availability is stable.

### Do I own my store, accounts and data?

Yes. Every store, ad account, marketplace account and analytics property is created in your business's name. We work with access you grant and can revoke.

### Is there a minimum contract?

Projects are one-time. Retainers run month to month with 30 days' notice, after an initial three-month period so there is time to see results. [CONFIRM WITH OWNER]

### Do your prices include GST?

No. All prices on this page are exclusive of 18% GST, which is added to our invoices.
