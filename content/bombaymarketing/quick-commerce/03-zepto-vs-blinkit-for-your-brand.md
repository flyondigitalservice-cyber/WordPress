# Zepto vs Blinkit for Your Brand: An Honest Comparison

Most comparisons of Zepto and Blinkit online quote commission percentages, onboarding timelines and order volumes that contradict each other, often on the same page. The reason is simple: neither platform publishes a rate card, and the platforms' own reach figures change every quarter. This guide compares the two on what can actually be checked, how each one onboards, charges, advertises and shares data, and tells you exactly where to find the numbers that cannot be checked from outside.

It is part of our [quick-commerce guide for Mumbai brands](/quick-commerce-agency-mumbai/).

## The short answer

- **Choose Blinkit first** if you want to start without waiting for a category manager, control your own listings and pricing from day one, and test one city before committing stock nationally.
- **Choose Zepto first** if you have a category where Zepto's team is actively looking for brands, you value a dedicated key account manager, and you are ready to negotiate commercial terms. Budget for its listing fee: about ₹30,000 per variant, GST included, which in our clients' experience does not come back as ad credit.
- **Do both** once your first platform shows a sell-through rate you can plan stock around. Running two quick-commerce channels from launch doubles your inventory exposure before you know which SKUs move.

The rest of this guide explains why.

## Onboarding: self-serve versus relationship-led

On Blinkit, the order of work is fixed: register, list, ship. You can complete it without speaking to anyone at Blinkit. That suits brands with clean documentation and a clear product, and it rewards preparation. Our [Blinkit seller onboarding checklist](/guides/blinkit-seller-onboarding-checklist/) lists every step.

On Zepto, the public site says little about the process beyond registration. Third-party guides commonly describe category managers reviewing applications and deciding which brands to onboard, but these are not Zepto's statements and they disagree on details. What this means for you: on Zepto, your pitch matters. A one-page brief with your current sales on other channels, your price against category leaders, your gross margin and your packaging photographs does more than any form field.

## Fees: what you can know before you sign

Blinkit lists seven charge types: Product ID activation, inwarding, storage, category commission, fulfilment, customer return and inventory removal. Exact amounts are visible only after login, in the commissions calculator.

Zepto does not publish its charge types. Expect to see some combination of commission or margin, fulfilment or logistics charges, and possibly fees linked to stock and marketing, but confirm the exact list in writing. [VERIFY every line in the Zepto term sheet.]

**The upfront fee per variant is the biggest difference at launch.** Our clients have paid about ₹25,000 per variant (GST included) on Blinkit and about ₹30,000 on Zepto. On Blinkit, the official FAQ says the activation fee is credited to your ads wallet, so it comes back as advertising. On Zepto it does not: in our clients' experience the fee is a cost that never comes back. That changes the comparison:

| 3 SKUs | Blinkit | Zepto |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cash | ₹75,000 | ₹90,000 |
| Returned as ad credit | ₹75,000 (per Blinkit FAQ) | ₹0 |
| Real cost | ₹0 net, if you would have spent it on ads anyway | ₹90,000, to be earned back from contribution |

If cash is tight, this alone can decide which platform you start on. For comparison, Swiggy Instamart has not charged our clients any onboarding fee; see [Swiggy Instamart vs your D2C website](/guides/swiggy-instamart-vs-d2c-website/).

**How to compare them properly:** do not compare commission percentages. Compare contribution per unit after all platform charges and the ad spend you expect to need, for the same SKU at the same selling price. Our [commissions and margins guide](/guides/quick-commerce-commissions-and-margins-india/) has a worksheet for this. Two platforms with different commission rates can produce the same profit once fulfilment, storage and ads are included.

## Model of sale: who owns the stock?

This question matters for tax, pricing control and risk, and it is often glossed over.

- **Marketplace model.** You own the inventory until a customer buys it. You set the price. The platform charges fees. Blinkit's Seller Hub describes this model: you send stock, Blinkit stores and fulfils it, you set the price and receive payouts net of fees.
- **Inventory or buy-sell model.** The platform, or an entity it works with, buys stock from you at an agreed price and resells it. You get paid on invoice terms, but you may lose control of the final selling price.

Which model applies to your Zepto or Instamart agreement depends on the contract. [VERIFY in your agreement: who invoices the customer, who holds title in the warehouse, and who sets the retail price.] Ask your chartered accountant to read this clause before signing; it changes how you account for the sale under GST.

## Advertising and visibility

Both platforms sell in-app advertising.

- Blinkit funds sponsored ads from an ads wallet, and the Product ID activation fee (about ₹25,000 per variant (GST included) for our clients [CONFIRM]) is credited to it, so every new product starts with a sizeable ad balance.
- Zepto states that its "custom ads increase your visibility".

Neither publishes minimum spends, formats or auction rules on its public pages. [VERIFY inside each ad console.] In our experience, the platform where you are better stocked will deliver a better return on the same ad budget, because an ad for a product that is unavailable in a store is wasted on every customer served by that store.

## Data and insight

Data is often the deciding factor for a growing brand. On quick commerce you do not get the customer's contact details on either platform; you get aggregated data.

- Blinkit's Seller Hub offers Keyword Analytics and shipment recommendations, which brands quoted on its own site use for range and stock planning.
- Zepto promises "full visibility into every transaction" and has publicised a data and insights platform for brands. [VERIFY the product's current name, what it shows and whether it is free or paid with your Zepto contact.]

Before signing with either, ask to see: city- and store-level sales, out-of-stock rate by store, search terms leading to your product, and ad performance by keyword. If a platform cannot show you store-level availability, you will be guessing at your stock plan.

## A decision checklist

Answer these before choosing.

1. Do you have GST, FSSAI (if food), trademark, barcodes and compliant packs ready? If yes, Blinkit self-serve is open to you now.
2. Is your category one where Zepto's selection looks thin in your target city? Check by searching the app in Mumbai. If thin, a pitch to Zepto is worth making.
3. Can you fund inventory for two platforms' warehouses at once? If not, start with one.
4. What is your contribution per unit on each platform after all fees and expected ads? Use real numbers from the dashboard and term sheet, not blog estimates.
5. Who sets the selling price under each agreement?
6. How soon do you get paid, and does that fit your working capital?

## Frequently asked questions

### Which has lower commission, Zepto or Blinkit?

Neither publishes a commission rate card, so any blanket claim is unverified. Blinkit shows SKU-level fees in its Seller Hub calculator; Zepto's terms are agreed per brand. Compare total contribution per unit, not headline commission.

### Can I sell the same product on both?

Yes, unless your agreement with either platform contains an exclusivity clause. [VERIFY in each agreement.] Keep pricing consistent; platforms monitor price parity and large gaps cause friction.

### Is it easier to get approved on Blinkit?

Blinkit's process is self-serve, with documents verified "in real time", so approval depends mainly on your paperwork. Zepto's process involves people, so it depends on your paperwork and your pitch.

### Does Zepto have a seller portal?

Yes. The Vendor Hub is at brands.zepto.co.in and registration at brands-onboarding.zepto.co.in.

### Which is bigger in Mumbai?

Neither publishes city-level customer or store counts on its seller pages. [VERIFY with each platform's category team, and check delivery coverage yourself by entering PIN codes in each app.]
