# Quick Commerce Packaging Requirements: A Practical Checklist

On quick commerce, your pack has three jobs. It must pass the platform's inwarding check at the warehouse. It must survive being picked, bagged and carried on a two-wheeler. And it must sell from a thumbnail the size of a postage stamp, because that is all the customer sees.

Most launch delays we see are not about documents. They are about packs: a missing barcode, a net quantity that does not match the listing, a pouch that leaks, a label that does not carry a mandatory declaration. This guide covers what is legally required, what platforms require on top, and what makes a pack sell. It is part of our [quick-commerce guide for Mumbai brands](/quick-commerce-agency-mumbai/).

## Layer 1: What the law requires on every retail pack

These apply whatever channel you sell through. Platforms check for them because the platform can be held responsible for listing non-compliant goods.

### Legal Metrology declarations

Pre-packaged commodities sold at retail in India must carry the declarations prescribed by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules. Commonly required declarations include:

- name and address of the manufacturer, packer or importer;
- country of origin for imported goods;
- common or generic name of the commodity;
- net quantity in standard units;
- month and year of manufacture (or import);
- retail sale price as MRP inclusive of all taxes;
- consumer care details: phone and email.

The rules have been amended several times, including changes to date marking and unit-sale-price requirements. [VERIFY the current mandatory list and font-size rules against the rules text via the [Legal Metrology portal](https://lm.doca.gov.in/) and the [Department of Consumer Affairs](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/).]

E-commerce entities are also required to display these declarations on the listing. That is why platforms ask for back-of-pack images, and why a mismatch between pack and listing gets flagged.

### FSSAI labelling (food, beverages, supplements)

Food products must also comply with FSSAI's Labelling and Display Regulations, listed on [FSSAI's regulations page](https://www.fssai.gov.in/cms/food-safety-and-standards-regulations.php). Core elements include:

- name of the food and list of ingredients in descending order;
- nutritional information;
- veg or non-veg symbol;
- allergen declaration;
- FSSAI logo and licence or registration number of the brand owner and, where different, the manufacturer;
- date marking: "Use by" or "Expiry" date, or "Best before", as applicable;
- batch or lot number;
- instructions for use and storage, where needed.

Front-of-pack nutrition labelling and other changes have been proposed and amended over time. [VERIFY the regulation version in force and any recent amendments on fssai.gov.in, and with your food technologist.] Your licence and registration details can be checked on [FoSCoS](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/).

### Plastic packaging obligations

Brand owners using plastic packaging have Extended Producer Responsibility obligations under the Plastic Waste Management Rules, including registration on CPCB's centralised EPR portal. ## Layer 2: What quick-commerce platforms require

### Barcode

Blinkit requires "a valid Unique Product Code (UPC)/barcode" to list a product; without one you can request an exception code via Help and Support, "subject to approval" ([Blinkit FAQ](https://seller.blinkit.com/faq)).

- Use GTINs registered to your company, issued by [GS1 India](https://www.gs1india.org/).
- One GTIN per distinct variant: size, flavour, pack count.
- Print the barcode where a picker's scanner can read it without unfolding or rotating the pack. Avoid curved seams, shiny foils behind the bars, and low contrast colour pairs.
- If you change the net quantity, you need a new GTIN.

### Match between pack and listing

Name, variant, net quantity, MRP and barcode on the pack must match the listing exactly. This is the single most common inwarding problem we see. Lock artwork and listing data together: when one changes, the other changes in the same sign-off.

### Shelf life at inwarding

Platforms reject food and other dated stock that arrives with too little shelf life remaining. The threshold is set by each platform and can vary by category. [VERIFY in each platform's inwarding guidelines.] Design implication: print dates clearly and in a consistent position so warehouse staff can check them quickly.

### Carton labelling

Outer cartons for inbound shipments typically need SKU, quantity, batch and expiry, and a shipment or appointment reference. Blinkit's shipment flow is: create a shipment, schedule a warehouse appointment, send stock ([Blinkit, How to sell](https://seller.blinkit.com/how-to-sell)). [VERIFY the exact carton label format in the shipment tool for Blinkit, and in Zepto's and Instamart's vendor documentation.]

### Product images

Platforms need front, back and supporting images. Back-of-pack images must make declarations legible. Image specifications live inside each listing tool. [VERIFY dimensions, background and count requirements per platform.]

## Layer 3: Transit and handling strength

A quick-commerce unit is handled many times: inbound cartons, warehouse storage, transfer to a dark store, shelving, picking, bagging with other items, and a ride. Packs that are fine on a supermarket shelf can fail here.

| Risk | What fails | Design fix |
|---|---|---|
| Crushing in a mixed bag | Thin cartons, unsupported pouches | Heavier board grade, gusseted or stand-up pouches, internal support |
| Leaks | Flip caps, screw caps without induction seal | Induction seals, tamper bands, secondary sleeve |
| Breakage | Glass without protection | Shrink sleeve, sleeve-and-tray, or switch to PET where suitable |
| Temperature | Chocolate, cosmetics, some beverages in Mumbai summer | Heat-resistant formulation or packaging; check whether the platform offers temperature control for your category [VERIFY] |
| Scuffing | Matte inks on high-touch surfaces | Varnish or lamination on high-wear areas |
| Barcode unreadable | Barcode on a seam or curve | Flat panel, quiet zone around the bars |

Seller-attributed returns cost money: Blinkit charges a customer return fee where the return reason is attributed to the seller ([Blinkit fees](https://seller.blinkit.com/fees-commission)). A leaking cap is a seller-attributed return. Packaging strength is a margin lever, which we cover in [quick-commerce commissions and margins](/guides/quick-commerce-commissions-and-margins-india/).

## Layer 4: Designing to sell from a thumbnail

A quick-commerce search results tile is tiny, and the customer scrolls fast. Packs designed for a physical shelf often become an unreadable blur.

- **One dominant element.** Brand or product name, large, high contrast. Not both competing.
- **Variant by colour.** Flavour or size should be identifiable by colour alone at thumbnail size.
- **Net quantity visible.** Shoppers compare value. If your net quantity is in small type, you lose the comparison.
- **Fewer claims on the front.** One clear claim reads; five do not.
- **Test on a phone.** Put your front-of-pack image at actual thumbnail size next to competitors' screenshots from the app. If you cannot read it at arm's length, nor can the customer.

## Pack architecture for quick-commerce economics

Platform fulfilment fees are typically charged per order, so very low price points struggle to make money. We worked through why in the [commissions and margins guide](/guides/quick-commerce-commissions-and-margins-india/).

- **Price-point packs.** Size the pack to the price you need, not the other way around.
- **Multipacks and combos.** Spread per-order fees across more value. Each combo needs its own GTIN and, because the fee is charged per variant, its own listing fee.
- **Trial sizes.** Useful for discovery, but model their economics first.
- **Fewer variants at launch.** Our clients have paid about ₹25,000 per variant (GST included) on Blinkit and ₹30,000 on Zepto and Instamart to go live [CONFIRM]. Because the fee is charged per variant, six flavours in two sizes means twelve fees: about ₹3 lakh on Blinkit and ₹3.6 lakh each on Zepto and Instamart. Design the launch range around your best two or three variants and add the rest once they sell.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need different packaging for quick commerce than for retail?

Not legally. The same declarations apply. Practically, many brands strengthen packs for handling and redesign the front for thumbnail legibility.

### Can I sell on Blinkit without a barcode?

Blinkit requires a barcode, but allows you to request an exception code through Help and Support, subject to approval. Do not plan a launch around the exception.

### Where do I get barcodes in India?

GS1 India issues GTINs registered to your company. Buying unregistered barcodes from resellers risks conflicts with other products in platform catalogues.

### How much shelf life must stock have when it reaches the platform?

It depends on the platform and category. [VERIFY in each platform's inwarding guidelines.]

### Do platforms check the label or just the listing?

Both. Listings are reviewed against your submitted images, and physical stock is checked at inwarding. Mismatches cause rejections at either point.
