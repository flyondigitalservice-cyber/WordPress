# Dark Store vs Own Warehouse: Choosing Your Fulfilment Model

When a brand joins a quick-commerce platform, it hands over part of its fulfilment. Stock goes to the platform's warehouse, the platform pushes it to dark stores, and a rider delivers it. The alternative is to keep stock in your own warehouse, or a third-party one you contract, and fulfil orders from your website or other channels yourself.

This guide explains how each model works, what it costs, and how to decide the mix. It is part of our [quick-commerce guide for Mumbai brands](/quick-commerce-agency-mumbai/).

## How platform dark-store fulfilment works

Blinkit's Seller Hub describes the flow in three steps: create a shipment, schedule a warehouse appointment, send stock. Blinkit then replenishes "across our dark stores" and "takes care of storage, fulfilment, and customer deliveries" ([Blinkit, How to sell](https://seller.blinkit.com/how-to-sell)).

Three things are worth understanding about that flow.

1. **You ship to a mother warehouse, not to each dark store.** The platform decides how to distribute stock across its stores. You influence it through the quantities you send and the cities you launch in.
2. **You pay to be in the network.** Blinkit lists per-unit inwarding, daily storage on unsold units, a fulfilment fee per order, and fees for seller-attributed returns and for removing inventory ([Blinkit fees](https://seller.blinkit.com/fees-commission)). Amounts are shown in Seller Hub. [VERIFY each amount for your SKUs.]
3. **The platform recommends what to send.** Blinkit sellers quoted on its site credit "shipment recommendations" with keeping products in stock. Treat recommendations as useful data, not a guarantee of sell-through.

Zepto and Instamart run comparable dark-store networks but do not publish their inbound process or charges publicly. [VERIFY inbound appointment rules, carton labelling and charges in your agreement or the vendor portal for each.]

## How own-warehouse fulfilment works

You hold stock at your own premises or with a third-party logistics provider (3PL). Orders from your website, marketplaces or B2B buyers are picked and packed there and handed to a courier or a hyperlocal delivery partner.

You control everything: packaging, inserts, dispatch cut-offs, which orders go first. You also own every cost and every failure.

If you store goods at an address not on your GST registration, it must be added as an additional place of business. [VERIFY on the [GST portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/) and with your chartered accountant; this also applies to 3PL warehouses.]

## Comparison

| Factor | Platform dark store | Own / 3PL warehouse |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery speed to customer | Minutes, within the dark store's radius | Courier: same-day in-city to several days nationally; hyperlocal partners can be faster in a small radius |
| Demand | Platform's app traffic | Your own channels |
| Cost structure | Mostly variable: per unit, per day, per order | Mix of fixed (rent, staff, software) and variable (packaging, courier) |
| Control over handling | Low | High |
| Packaging requirements | Platform inwarding and QC rules | Your own standards plus courier requirements |
| Inventory visibility | Via platform dashboard | Your own WMS or spreadsheets |
| Capital tied up | Stock in platform network across cities | Stock in one or a few locations |
| Risk of write-off | Expiry or damage in network; removal fees to recover it | Expiry or damage in your warehouse |
| Customer data | Aggregated only | Full, for own-website orders |
| Scalability | Add cities by shipping to new warehouses | Add capacity, staff, possibly locations |

| Customer acquisition | In-app ads | Your own ads |
| Upfront fee per product | About ₹25,000 (Blinkit, returned as ad credit) to ₹30,000 (Zepto, Instamart) in our clients' onboarding [CONFIRM] | None |

On those assumptions, storage is far more expensive per unit in dark stores and delivery is far cheaper. That is the typical shape, not a law: dark-store networks charge for scarce space close to customers and save on the last mile. The decisive number is how many days your stock sits before it sells.

**Rule of thumb from the worksheet:** if a SKU sells slowly, the dark-store storage line grows until it eats the delivery saving. The per-product listing fee pushes the same way. A slow SKU must earn back both storage and a ₹25,000–₹30,000 fee, which is one more reason to keep long-tail variants in your own warehouse. Fast sellers belong in dark stores; slow sellers, large packs and long-tail variants usually belong in your own warehouse.

## Inventory planning across both

Running both models means splitting stock. Practical rules we use with clients:

1. **Allocate by velocity.** Send dark stores only the SKUs and quantities that sell within your target days-of-cover.
2. **Keep a reserve.** Hold safety stock at your own warehouse to replenish platform shipments quickly when a city runs low.
3. **Watch shelf life.** Stock at the platform has to clear inwarding with enough life remaining and then sell before expiry. Platforms set minimum remaining shelf life at inwarding. [VERIFY the threshold in each platform's inwarding guidelines.] FSSAI has also issued directions on shelf life for food sold through e-commerce. [VERIFY current requirements on [fssai.gov.in](https://www.fssai.gov.in/).]
4. **Plan removals.** Blinkit charges an inventory removal fee per unit to pull stock out. Before you send seasonal stock, decide what happens to the leftovers.
5. **Reconcile monthly.** Compare units shipped, units received (as recorded at inwarding), units sold, units returned and units on hand. Discrepancies should be raised through the platform's support process promptly. [VERIFY the claim window in your agreement.]

## When your own warehouse is the better choice

- Large or heavy items where dark-store storage per unit is high.
- Low-velocity SKUs, long-tail variants and gift sets.
- Products needing careful handling, such as glass, or temperature control the platform cannot guarantee for your category. [VERIFY cold-chain availability with each platform.]
- Subscription and bundle orders from your website.
- B2B and bulk orders.

## When dark stores are the better choice

- Fast-moving impulse and daily-use items.
- Products where speed is the purchase reason.
- Launch-phase testing in a metro, before investing in your own demand generation.
- Brands without warehouse staff or systems who want to start selling quickly.

## Questions to ask before choosing a 3PL in Mumbai

If you go the own-warehouse route via a 3PL, ask for:

- a rate card covering receiving, storage, pick, pack, packaging materials and returns processing;
- their GST registration and willingness to be added as your additional place of business;
- integration with your store platform and marketplaces;
- dispatch cut-off times and same-day capacity;
- insurance cover for stock in their custody;
- whether they can prepare and label cartons for quick-commerce warehouse appointments, which saves a separate handling step.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a dark store?

A small warehouse, closed to walk-in shoppers, located close to customers so that orders can be picked and delivered in minutes. On quick-commerce platforms, brands do not ship directly to dark stores; they ship to the platform's warehouse, which replenishes the stores.

### Can I deliver quick-commerce orders from my own warehouse?

Not on the major platforms' standard models, which fulfil from their own networks. For speed from your own stock, look at hyperlocal delivery partners for your website orders.

### How much does dark-store storage cost?

Blinkit charges a daily storage fee per unsold unit and shows the amount in Seller Hub. It does not publish the rate. Zepto and Instamart terms are in their agreements. [VERIFY]

### Is a 3PL cheaper than my own warehouse?

It depends on your volume. A 3PL converts fixed costs (rent, staff) into variable ones. At low volumes that is usually cheaper; at high, steady volumes your own facility can win. Compare on cost per order shipped.

### What happens to unsold stock in a dark store?

It keeps incurring storage fees until it sells or you request removal, which on Blinkit carries a per-unit removal fee. Expired stock is a loss. Plan quantities around sell-through, not around the minimum order the factory gave you.
