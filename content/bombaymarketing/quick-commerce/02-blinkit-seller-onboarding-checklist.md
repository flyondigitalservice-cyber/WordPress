# Blinkit Seller Onboarding Checklist

Blinkit is the only one of India's big three quick-commerce apps with a public, self-serve seller programme. Its Seller Hub publishes the steps, the documents and the fee structure, which makes it the most predictable place for a new brand to start. This checklist follows Blinkit's own published process, adds the preparation work that the official pages do not spell out, and marks every figure we could not verify.

It is part of our [quick-commerce guide for Mumbai brands](/quick-commerce-agency-mumbai/). Read that first if you are still deciding whether the channel suits you.

## What Blinkit itself says

Before the checklist, here is what Blinkit publishes, quoted from its Seller Hub as checked on 15 November 2026. Platform pages change; re-read them before you apply.

- Seller Hub is "a self-serve platform that lets you onboard, list products, manage inventory, and sell on Blinkit" ([Blinkit Seller Hub FAQ](https://seller.blinkit.com/faq)).
- Selling starts in three steps: register on Seller Hub, create your product listing, ship to Blinkit ([How to sell](https://seller.blinkit.com/how-to-sell)).
- Onboarding "is completed in real time once your required documents are successfully verified", and you can "start selling in as little as 7 days".
- There is no registration fee. A Product ID activation fee applies when you activate a product, and the amount is credited to your ads wallet. Blinkit does not publish the amount; our clients have been charged about ₹25,000 per product. [CONFIRM current amount, whether GST is added, and whether each variant counts as a separate product.]
- You control your selling price and can change it from the dashboard.
- Payouts run on two cycles a month, the 1st to 15th and 16th to month end, processed on the third working day after each cycle.

Note what the "7 days" claim covers. It is Blinkit's best case from registration to first sale. It does not include the time you spend getting a GST registration, an FSSAI licence, barcodes or compliant packaging. For a brand starting from zero on those, the preparation is the long part.

## Stage 1: Business and tax documents

Blinkit's published list is short: GST certificate, bank account details, brand name, manufacturer details and logo, FSSAI licence where applicable, and a digital signature.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **GST registration certificate.** Download a current copy from the [GST portal](https://www.gst.gov.in/). Confirm the registration is active and that the trade name and legal name are what you expect using the public [taxpayer search](https://services.gst.gov.in/services/searchtp).
- [ ] **Principal and additional places of business are current.** If you store goods at an address that is not on your GST registration, fix that first. Blinkit states you can sell across its cities "with a single GST and bank account". Whether you ever need to add a Blinkit warehouse as an additional place of business depends on the selling model and who holds title to the stock. [VERIFY with your chartered accountant and in the Seller Hub onboarding terms.]
- [ ] **Bank account in the same legal name as the GSTIN.** Keep a cancelled cheque or bank statement ready. Blinkit's page says "bank account details"; the exact proof requested appears in the registration flow. [VERIFY in Seller Hub.]
- [ ] **Digital signature.** Blinkit lists this explicitly. It is used to sign the seller agreement. If your authorised signatory does not have a Class 3 digital signature certificate, get one issued before you start. [VERIFY the accepted signature type in the Seller Hub registration screen.]
- [ ] **PAN of the business.** Not on Blinkit's published list, but it is embedded in your GSTIN and commonly requested by platforms. Keep it ready.

New to GST? The portal's own [registration user guide](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/registration/index.htm) walks through the application. Approval time varies by state and by whether your application is flagged for physical verification, so do not plan a launch date around it.

## Stage 2: Category licences

**Food, beverages and supplements.** Blinkit asks for an FSSAI licence "where applicable". Every food business operator needs either a registration or a licence, issued through FSSAI's [FoSCoS portal](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/). Which tier you need depends on turnover and activity, and the turnover thresholds are set by FSSAI. [VERIFY the current thresholds on FoSCoS, which shows eligibility during the application.]

- [ ] The licence covers your **kind of business**. Manufacturer, marketer, relabeller and trader are separate activities. A brand that gets its product contract-manufactured usually needs the correct endorsement as a marketer, and the manufacturer needs its own licence.
- [ ] The licence covers your **product category**. Check the product list on the licence itself.
- [ ] The licence is **valid past your planned launch** by a comfortable margin.
- [ ] The **licence number on your pack** matches the document you upload.

**Other categories.** Cosmetics, Ayurvedic products, electronics, toys and some packaged goods have their own approvals, for example a cosmetics manufacturing licence or a BIS certification. Blinkit's public pages do not list these per category. [VERIFY category-specific requirements in Seller Hub's listing flow or by raising a Help and Support ticket.]

## Stage 3: Brand proof

Blinkit's FAQ states you can sell multiple brands through one account "provided you have the required brand trademark and authorizations".

- [ ] **Trademark registration certificate or application.** Search and file through [IP India](https://ipindia.gov.in/). Whether a pending application is accepted in place of a registered mark is not stated publicly. [VERIFY in the Seller Hub brand registration step.]
- [ ] **Brand authorisation letter** if you are a distributor or reseller, on the brand owner's letterhead, signed and dated.
- [ ] **Logo file** in a clean, high-resolution format. Blinkit lists the logo as a registration requirement.
- [ ] **Manufacturer details**: name, address and licence numbers of whoever makes the product.

## Stage 4: Product data

Blinkit's published requirement: product name and details, images, videos and other media, pricing and taxation details, plus "a valid Unique Product Code (UPC)/barcode". If you have no barcode, you "can request an exception code by raising a ticket through Help & Support, subject to approval".

- [ ] **One barcode per sellable variant.** Barcodes registered in your company's name are issued by [GS1 India](https://www.gs1india.org/). Do not depend on the exception route for a launch; it is discretionary.
- [ ] **Barcode printed on pack and scannable**, not only stored in a spreadsheet.
- [ ] **Titles** in a consistent pattern: brand, product, variant, net quantity.
- [ ] **HSN code and GST rate per SKU**, matching your invoices.
- [ ] **MRP per SKU**, matching the pack.
- [ ] **Pack dimensions and weight.** Blinkit's fee description says commission varies by category and pricing; fee calculators inside the hub also ask for physical attributes. [VERIFY which attributes drive which fee in the commissions calculator.]
- [ ] **Images**: front of pack on a clean background, back of pack showing ingredients and declarations, and one image that shows scale. Image size, count and background rules are in the listing tool. [VERIFY specifications in Seller Hub.]
- [ ] **Shelf life in days** for anything perishable or dated.

## Stage 5: Packaging and label compliance

The listing can be approved while the physical stock still gets refused at the warehouse. Check labels against the law, not against the platform.

- [ ] **Packaged commodity declarations**: manufacturer or packer name and address, common name of the product, net quantity, month and year of manufacture, MRP inclusive of all taxes, and consumer care contact. These come from the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, available via the [Legal Metrology portal](https://lm.doca.gov.in/). [VERIFY the current mandatory declarations with the rules text; they have been amended several times.]
- [ ] **Food labelling**: ingredients, nutrition information, allergen declaration, veg or non-veg mark, FSSAI logo and licence number, and date marking, under the Labelling and Display Regulations listed on [FSSAI's regulations page](https://www.fssai.gov.in/cms/food-safety-and-standards-regulations.php).
- [ ] **Remaining shelf life at inwarding.** Platforms set a minimum, and FSSAI has issued directions to e-commerce food businesses on minimum shelf life at delivery. [VERIFY the current threshold in Blinkit's inwarding guidelines and on FSSAI's advisories page.]
- [ ] **Outer cartons** labelled with SKU, quantity, batch and expiry, in the format the shipment tool specifies. [VERIFY in the shipment creation screen.]

## Stage 6: Register, list, ship

- [ ] **Register on Seller Hub** at [seller.blinkit.com](https://seller.blinkit.com/) with the documents above.
- [ ] **Create listings** and choose launch cities. Blinkit lets you launch in "your preferred city", which means you can test Mumbai before committing stock nationally.
- [ ] **Budget for and pay the Product ID activation fee** for each product. Our clients have paid about ₹25,000 per product, credited back to the ads wallet. Five products is about ₹1.25 lakh before any stock moves. The current amount is shown in the hub. [CONFIRM in Seller Hub before activating.]
- [ ] **Decide which products to activate first.** Start with your two or three strongest SKUs. Each activation is a separate fee, so a full-range launch multiplies the upfront cost.
- [ ] **Plan how to spend the ad credit** in the cities where you have stock, rather than leaving it to sit in the wallet.
- [ ] **Open the commissions calculator** and record the fee for each SKU at your intended price. This is the step most sellers skip.
- [ ] **Create a shipment**, using the hub's quantity recommendations as a starting point.
- [ ] **Schedule a warehouse appointment.**
- [ ] **Dispatch** with invoice, e-way bill where required, and the shipment documents the hub generates. [VERIFY e-way bill applicability with your accountant; it depends on consignment value and movement type.]
- [ ] **Confirm inwarding** in the dashboard and check the received quantity against what you sent.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a fee to become a Blinkit seller?

There is no fee to register. There is a fee for each product you activate: Blinkit calls it the Product ID activation fee and credits it to your ads wallet for sponsored ads. Our clients have paid about ₹25,000 per product. [CONFIRM the current amount in Seller Hub.]

### Can I sell on Blinkit without a barcode?

Blinkit asks for a valid UPC or barcode. Without one you can request an exception code through Help and Support, and approval is at Blinkit's discretion.

### Do I need a separate GST registration for each city?

Blinkit's Seller Hub says you can start selling in 300+ cities "with a single GST and bank account". Confirm the state-wise tax position for your own structure with your accountant.

### Who sets the price?

You do. Blinkit says sellers have "complete control" over listing price and can update it at any time.

### How and when does Blinkit pay?

Automatically to your registered bank account, net of fees, on the third working day after each cycle. Cycles run from the 1st to the 15th and the 16th to month end.

### Can an agency register on my behalf?

The account must be in your business's name with your documents and digital signature. Seller Hub's Access Management lets you invite team members with role-based access, which is how an agency should work on your account. Our [Blinkit seller onboarding service](/blinkit-seller-onboarding/) operates this way.
