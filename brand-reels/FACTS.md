# Facts used in the films (check before posting)

## Stanley
- Founded 1913 (William Stanley Jr.); known for rugged green vacuum flasks.
- Revenue about $73M (2019) to about $750M (2023): widely reported, e.g. CNBC, Jan 2024.
- The Quencher had weak sales and was nearly discontinued; The Buy Guide (three women: Ashlee LeSueur, Taylor Cannon, Linzy Bryson) bought 5,000 units to sell to their followers in 2019 and they sold out. Reported by CNBC and others.
- Terence Reilly (ex-Crocs) took over marketing and leaned into colourways, limited drops and women customers.
- Nov 2023: a TikTok showed a burnt car with a Stanley cup still holding ice; Stanley's president offered to replace her car.

## Scrub Daddy
- Inventor Aaron Krause (car-detailing buffing pads); foam that is firm in cold water and soft in warm water.
- Shark Tank 2012 deal with Lori Greiner; it sold tens of thousands of units on QVC in minutes.
- Often called the most successful Shark Tank product; sales in the hundreds of millions (reported).

## Poppi
- Founders Allison and Stephen Ellsworth, Texas; started as "Mother Beverage", an apple-cider-vinegar drink.
- Shark Tank deal (aired 2018) with Rohan Oza; rebranded to Poppi.
- Grew through TikTok; PepsiCo announced in March 2025 that it would acquire Poppi for about $1.95B (the figure includes about $300M of expected tax benefits).

## Liquid Death
- Founder Mike Cessario, a former ad creative; noticed performers drinking water from energy-drink cans at festivals.
- Water in tall-boy cans, punk/heavy-metal branding, slogan "Murder Your Thirst"; launched 2019.
- Made a low-budget video ad before the product existed; it drew millions of views (company account, widely reported).
- Stunts such as albums made from hate comments. March 2024 funding round valued it at about $1.4B (reported).

## Rhode
- Hailey Bieber launched Rhode in June 2022 with three products.
- Phone case with a slot for a lip tint became a social-media status item; small drops sold out.
- May 2025: e.l.f. Beauty agreed to acquire Rhode for up to $1B ($800M plus up to $200M earn-out); Rhode had about $212M in trailing sales.

## Labubu (shown with a generic stand-in toy, not the real character design)
- Created by artist Kasing Lung for his picture-book series The Monsters; licensed by Pop Mart, sold in blind boxes.
- Popularity surged after BLACKPINK's Lisa posted with it in 2024; charms on luxury bags, queues, scuffles and high resale prices were widely reported.
- Pop Mart's revenue and share price rose sharply in 2024–25.
