# Brand Story Reels — Treatment (Pop Art series)

Series: 1-minute vertical (1080×1920, 24 fps) Instagram Reels telling how product brands won.
Style: Pop Art (screen-print, Ben-Day dots fixed to paper, red / yellow / blue + black key on warm paper).
Voice: Hinglish narration in Hindi (offline Kokoro voice `hm_omega`, Devanagari text so English loanwords are pronounced Hindi-style).
Subtitles: Roman Hinglish (how Indian Instagram reads), burned in on a paper slip, also exported as `.srt`.
Handle watermark: one variable (`HANDLE` in each film's `content.js`), top-right slip and end card.
Sound: soft print-shop foley only (stamp thump, paper whoosh, card flick). **No pen, pencil or squeegee scratching**, per the brief. 60s lounge score: vibraphone, marimba, jazz bass, conga, shaker, claps, muted trumpet.

Rights: brand names are said and set in our own type (telling a factual story). No logos, real packaging or photos are drawn. Every product is a generic printed stand-in (a tumbler, a smiley sponge, a soda can).

## Three candidate series structures
1. **Countdown of posters**: five numbered posters per story, each printed plate by plate. Clear, but every film looks the same, and the count doesn't help these stories.
2. **Before / after wall**: the same wall of copies in two colourways. Strong for one beat, too thin for 60 s.
3. **One print becomes a market (chosen)**: each film follows one product print. It starts alone on an empty sheet, gets into trouble (dots darken, a front spreads), finds its trick (copies stamp outward, a colourway flips), and ends on a big printed number entered through its counter, then a lesson slip. It has one subject, one turn and an echo, and it suits a "one product, one clever move" story.

## Shared grammar (all three films)
- **Opening (≤3 s)**: the hook number is printed in, colour plates sliding into register on the first downbeat, and the product print stamped under it. There is never a blank frame.
- **Labels**: paper slip top-left (year, name). **Subtitles**: paper slip bottom-centre, ≤2 lines.
- **Transitions only from print**: stamp, card flip, push through a round shape, a label sliding out, a lateral track. No fades and no dissolves.
- **Ending**: a lesson slip, then an end card with the handle and "FOLLOW", on a wall of the film's product with one tile in a different colourway.
- **Silence**: each film holds about 0.8 s on a low note before its turn; the first sound after it is a stamp or a flip.

## Benchmark
- Learn from: 1960s title sequences and print-ad posters (locked big condensed type, flat primaries, repetition), and the pacing of short business-explainer Reels (a number in the first second, one idea per screen).
- Don't take: any poster's artwork, any brand's packaging, logos, typefaces or jingles.

## Film 1 — STANLEY: "Asli customer koi aur tha" (~58 s)
**Logline:** A 110-year-old flask brand nearly drops a tumbler nobody buys, until women online adopt it and a burnt car proves it.
**Arc:** setup (rugged green flask for workers) → turn (bloggers buy 5,000 cups; pastel colourways) → proof (car fire, ice still inside) → ending ($73M → $750M; the lesson).

| # | Time | Framing / camera | What we see | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4.5 | Full; slow push | "$73M → $750M" printed in, tumbler stamped below | the hook number first |
| 2 | 4.5–7 | Medium; pull | title "STANLEY" printed, label "KAHANI" | names the subject |
| 3 | 7–13 | Lateral track | green flask print, worker's lunchbox and tent shapes beside it, label "1913" | sequence = old world |
| 4 | 13–21 | Close → slow push | lone tumbler; a front of red+blue dots darkens it (not selling) | threat = spread |
| 5 | 21–29 | Pull to wall | three paper "post" cards, then the tumbler stamped in rings faster and faster into a wall, label "5,000 CUPS" | copies = selling out |
| 6 | 29–36 | Wall, flip wave | tiles flip from green to pastel colourways (pink, sky, lemon) | substitution = new customer |
| 7 | 36–45 | Medium; push | car print; a red/yellow front (fire) sweeps across it; tumbler in the seat stays blue with ice cubes | signature: dots as fire |
| 8 | 45–49 | Full | car flips to a new car with a bow | the gift |
| 9 | 49–54 | Push through the "0" of 750 | bar chart: small 2019 bar, tall 2023 bar | scale |
| 10 | 54–58.5 | Wall with one odd tile; end card | lesson slip, handle | echo and CTA |

Beat sheet = shot times above. Cue map: 104 bpm (2.31 s per bar); sections intro (bars 1–2), verse (3–9, bass + shaker), turn (silence before bar 10, then claps come in), fire (bowed vibes plus bass pedal only), payoff (full groove + muted trumpet), end (vibes chord). Stamps fall on downbeats.
Sound table: intro = stamp + vibes sting; story = paper whoosh on camera moves; dark front = low crackle bed; flips = card flick, pitched marimba note climbing per flip; fire = soft roar bed (filtered noise), no music groove; payoff = claps.

## Film 2 — SCRUB DADDY: "Boring product ko chehra do" (~56 s)
**Logline:** A car-polish inventor's foam that goes hard in cold water and soft in warm becomes a smiley sponge, and TV turns it into a giant brand.
**Arc:** a sponge that hardens and softens with temperature (cold = blue plate, warm = red plate), the smiley face cut-out (the turn), a TV deal (2012), a wall of smiley sponges, then "hundreds of millions" in sales.
Shots: hook "SHARK TANK KA SABSE BADA HIT?" stamped over the sponge → foam block in blue (hard) / red (soft) split, flip → the face is cut (eyes, then mouth, printed plate by plate) → a TV-screen print with "2012" → pull from one sponge to a wall of sponges → lesson slip → end card.

## Film 3 — POPPI: "Naam badlo, kismat badlo" (~57 s)
**Logline:** An apple-cider-vinegar drink called "Mother Beverage" gets a new name and bright cans, then TikTok, then PepsiCo pays about $1.95B.
**Arc:** the kitchen drink, the ugly name crossed out (the turn: card flip to "POPPI"), cans stamped in colourways, phone-screen prints multiplying (TikTok), then the "$1.95B" number entered through its counter.

## Subtitle and title design
- Headlines: Anton, 140–240 px, colour fill + black outline + offset shadow in another plate.
- Labels: Archivo Black on a paper slip with a black frame and a coloured offset block, tilted ±2°.
- Subtitles: Archivo Black 46 px, Roman Hinglish, ≤2 lines, on a paper slip at the bottom centre (above the Reels UI safe zone: bottom 15% is kept clear).
- Safe zones for Reels: keep text inside x 80–1000, y 220–1560.
