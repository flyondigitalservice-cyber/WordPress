A warranty is the main thing that separates a renewed laptop from a risky second hand one. But warranties on refurbished laptops in the UAE range from nothing at all to several months, and the details matter. Here is what a refurbished laptop warranty normally covers, and how Posh Laptop's works.

Typical warranty lengths in the UAE

Always read the period stated for the specific product, because some items such as accessories may have different terms.

What a warranty usually covers

  • Hardware faults that appear during normal use: motherboard, RAM, SSD, screen, keyboard, ports
  • Faults present at the time of sale that were not disclosed
  • Repair, replacement with an equivalent unit, or refund, depending on the seller's terms

What is usually not covered

Our own terms, like most sellers', exclude:

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Accidental damage, drops and liquid spills
  • Misuse or abnormal working and storage conditions
  • Repairs or modifications by someone other than the seller

Batteries are consumable parts, so ask specifically how the battery is covered and what its health was at the time of sale.

7 questions to ask before you buy

  1. How long is the warranty, and does it start from purchase or delivery?
  2. Is it written on the invoice?
  3. Is it repair only, or replacement and refund too?
  4. Who pays for collection or delivery during a claim?
  5. Is the battery covered, and what is its current health?
  6. Is there a return window if I simply change my mind?
  7. Can I upgrade RAM or SSD without voiding the warranty?

Frequently asked questions

How long is the warranty on a refurbished laptop in the UAE?

It depends on the seller: from none for private sales up to several months. Posh Laptop gives 6 months from delivery on renewed laptops, plus a 15-day return window on online orders.

Does the warranty cover the battery?

Batteries are consumable, so cover varies. Ask the seller for the battery health at purchase and how battery faults are handled under the warranty.

Does a warranty cover accidental damage?

Usually not. Accidental damage, drops, liquid spills and repairs by third parties are excluded from most refurbished laptop warranties, including ours.

Can I return a refurbished laptop if I change my mind?

Posh Laptop accepts returns within 15 days of receipt on online orders, in original condition with accessories and packaging.
