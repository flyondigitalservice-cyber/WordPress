Which Chromebook should you buy?

For school and homework

Pick a model with 4GB RAM and 32GB storage, such as the HP Chromebook 11 G7 EE, HP 11 G8 or Dell Chromebook 3100. They run Google Classroom, Docs, Meet and the browser comfortably, and the education (EE) versions are built to survive a school bag. Browse all Chromebooks for school in the UAE.

For the lowest budget

The Lenovo Chromebook N22 at AED 199 and the 2GB Dell models are fine for browsing, video calls and online documents. If the laptop will also be used for many open tabs, step up to 4GB RAM.

For touch and drawing

The Acer Chromebook R11 and Dell Chromebook 11 3189 fold into tablet mode, which younger students find easier for reading and drawing apps.

Chromebook or Windows laptop?

A Chromebook boots in seconds, updates itself and is hard to slow down with viruses, which makes it ideal for children and light users. If you need Microsoft Office desktop apps, school software that only runs on Windows, or more storage, look at our student laptops or the wider range of affordable laptops in the UAE. Before buying any Chromebook, check its auto-update date on Google's support site so you know how long it will keep getting Chrome OS updates.
