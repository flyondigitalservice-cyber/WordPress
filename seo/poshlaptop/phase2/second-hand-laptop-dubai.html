Checklist before you buy any second hand laptop

  • Battery health: ask for the battery report or cycle count, not just "it charges"
  • Screen: look for dead pixels, pressure marks and backlight bleed on a white and a black background
  • Keyboard and ports: test every key, both USB ports, HDMI and the charger port
  • Storage: insist on an SSD; a hard drive makes even a fast processor feel slow
  • Processor generation: for Windows 11, Intel 8th Gen or newer is the safe minimum
  • Legal Windows licence and a clean install, with no previous owner's accounts
  • Warranty and returns in writing
