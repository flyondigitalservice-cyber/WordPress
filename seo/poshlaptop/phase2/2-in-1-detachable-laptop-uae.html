Detachable vs convertible: which type do you need?

Detachables (Dell Latitude 7320, 7210, 5285, HP Elite x2, Surface Book) let you pull the screen off the keyboard and use it as a light tablet. Convertibles (such as the HP EliteBook x360 and Lenovo ThinkPad Yoga) keep the keyboard attached and fold back 360°. Detachables are lighter as tablets; convertibles feel sturdier on your lap.
