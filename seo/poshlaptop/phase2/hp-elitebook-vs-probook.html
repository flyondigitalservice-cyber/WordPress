Which should you buy?

  • Choose a ProBook if price matters most and the laptop will mostly stay on a desk or in a bag between home and office.
  • Choose an EliteBook if you travel, present to clients, or want a lighter, better-built laptop with a nicer screen and keyboard.
  • Generation matters more than the badge: an EliteBook 840 G8 (11th Gen) will feel faster than an older ProBook 440 G5. Aim for Intel 8th Gen or newer for Windows 11.
  • 16GB RAM is worth it if you use many browser tabs, Teams and Excel together.

Frequently asked questions

Is HP EliteBook better than ProBook?

EliteBook is HP's premium business line with better build quality, displays and security features. ProBook is the more affordable line. For the same processor generation, an EliteBook is the better laptop but costs more.

Is the HP ProBook 430 G8 good?

Yes. The ProBook 430 G8 is a compact 13.3-inch business laptop with Intel 11th Gen processors and Windows 11 support, a good choice for students and office staff on a budget.

What is the difference between EliteBook 830 and 840?

The EliteBook 830 has a 13.3-inch screen and is smaller and lighter. The 840 has a 14-inch screen with similar internals. Choose the 830 for travel and the 840 for a slightly larger workspace.

Do renewed HP laptops support Windows 11?

HP ProBook and EliteBook models with Intel 8th Gen processors or newer generally support Windows 11. Ask us to confirm for a specific model.
