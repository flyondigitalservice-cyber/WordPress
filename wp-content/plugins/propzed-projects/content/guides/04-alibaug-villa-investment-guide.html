Alibaug has been Mumbai's favourite coastal escape for decades: beaches, coconut and mango orchards, and old bungalows a short ferry ride from the Gateway of India. Atal Setu (the Mumbai Trans Harbour Link) has now made it reachable by road in far less time, and demand for villas has followed. This guide explains where to buy, what rules apply on the coast and how to check a property properly.

Getting there: why Alibaug is now closer

  • Atal Setu: the sea bridge from Sewri to Nhava Sheva, opened in 2024, cuts the road trip from South and Central Mumbai to Alibaug significantly. Many owners now drive down for a day.
  • Ferries: passenger catamarans from the Gateway of India to Mandwa, and the RoRo ferry that takes your car from Mumbai's Ferry Wharf (Bhaucha Dhakka) to Mandwa, both operating in fair weather.
  • Navi Mumbai airport: the new airport is within easy driving distance, useful for owners and for holiday-rental guests.

Where to buy in Alibaug

  • Mandwa, Kihim and Awas: closest to the jetty, with established bungalow belts and some of the highest prices. Ideal if you plan to use the ferry.
  • Nagaon, Akshi and Varsoli: beach villages south and north of Alibaug town, popular for holiday homes and rentals.
  • Alibaug town outskirts and the Pen road: more affordable villa projects and plots, with easier access to services and the highway.
  • Revdanda and Kashid side: quieter beaches and larger land parcels, further from the jetty.

Coastal rules you must know

CRZ (Coastal Regulation Zone): land close to the high-tide line falls under CRZ rules that restrict or prohibit new construction. A "sea-facing plot" may not allow the villa you imagine. Ask for the CRZ status and the distance from the high-tide line, and check any existing structure was built with permission.

Agricultural land: as everywhere in Maharashtra, only registered agriculturists can buy agricultural land. Non-farmers should buy NA plots or villas on NA land with building permission.

Old bungalows: many Alibaug properties have long family histories. A 30-year title search, all mutation entries and the consent of every legal heir are essential.

Returns: lifestyle plus rental

Alibaug has one of the strongest holiday-rental markets around Mumbai, driven by weekend and festival demand. Villas with a pool, garden and short access from the jetty or Atal Setu route do best. Plan for higher upkeep near the sea (salt air, monsoon damage) and use a professional manager if you want steady bookings.

Due-diligence checklist for Alibaug property

  1. 7/12 extract and 30 years of mutation entries; consent of all legal heirs for family property.
  2. NA order for residential use covering the exact survey number.
  3. CRZ status and distance from the high-tide line; permissions for existing structures.
  4. Approved layout, building permission and completion certificate for villas.
  5. MahaRERA registration for villa projects that require it.
  6. Legal access road, water source and electricity connection.
  7. Stamp duty at the applicable local rate plus 1% registration capped at ₹30,000.

FAQs

How long does it take to reach Alibaug from Mumbai?

By catamaran from the Gateway of India to Mandwa, about an hour on the water plus the onward road trip. By road via Atal Setu, many trips from South and Central Mumbai now take well under two hours depending on traffic. The RoRo ferry carries cars from Ferry Wharf to Mandwa.

Can I build a villa on a sea-facing plot in Alibaug?

Only if the plot is outside the no-development part of the Coastal Regulation Zone and has NA and building permissions. Check the CRZ classification and distance from the high-tide line before buying.

Is Alibaug a good place to invest in a villa?

Alibaug combines a strong weekend-rental market with better road access since Atal Setu. Returns depend on location, legal title and management. Buy clean-title villas with proper permissions.

Can a non-farmer buy land in Alibaug?

Not agricultural land. Non-farmers can buy NA (non-agricultural) plots or villas built on NA land with valid permissions.
