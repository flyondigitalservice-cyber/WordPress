=== Boutique Desk ===
Requires Plugins: woocommerce
Stable tag: 1.1.0

Limited staff dashboard plus GST invoices and packing slips for WooCommerce clothing stores.

== What it does ==
* Category Sellers (1.1): outside sellers list products only in the categories you assign (Jewellery, Bags, Accessories...), see only their own products, images and order lines, and get a Seller Desk with revenue, earnings after commission, a 30-day chart, best sellers and sold-out items. Optional product review before going live. WooCommerce → Sellers shows every seller's sales and payable amount.
* Store Editor role: add and edit products, process orders, print documents. No deleting, no settings, no plugins, themes, users, coupons or analytics.
* Store Desk dashboard: sales today and this month, orders to pack, 14-day sales chart, best sellers, sold-out products, one-click "Mark shipped".
* Tax invoice: sequential numbers, GSTIN, HSN per product, place of supply, size/colour/fabric per item, amount in words (Indian numbering).
* Packing slip: product photos, sizes, packing checkboxes, big "COD: collect ₹X" box, return address and care note.
* Bulk print from the Orders list; customers can download invoices from My account.
* Print or save as PDF from the browser; no PDF library needed.

== Setup ==
1. Upload and activate.
2. WooCommerce → Invoices & slips: add store address, GSTIN, logo, invoice prefix.
3. Users → Add New → role "Store Editor" for staff.
4. For outside sellers: Users → Add New → role "Category Seller", then on their profile tick their categories and set the commission %.
